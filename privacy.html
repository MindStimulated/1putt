Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to fulfill your order and support the product: your name, shipping address, email address, and order details. Payments are processed by our payment processor — we never see or store your card number.

If you join our email list, we use your address to send putting tips and product news. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and we don't share your address with third parties.

We use standard analytics to understand how the site is used (pages visited, approximate location, device type) so we can improve it. You can block analytics cookies in your browser at any time without affecting your ability to shop.

To review, correct, or delete your personal information, contact us and we'll respond within 30 days.
